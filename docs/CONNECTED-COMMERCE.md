# Connected commerce preview

The three clients share the same .NET10/ABP application services and persistent database. Open the wholesaler, then its merchant and courier links in the same browser; use a bounded shop invitation for another tester. The owner workspace capability remains private. `/live-work.html` presents the actual merchant and wholesaler interfaces together.

## Test a direct order

1. In the merchant catalogue, choose a product with a published price. Demo offers are labelled fictional. Search, all 13 catalogue categories, brand search, stock filters, sorting and exact barcode lookup operate on the actual catalogue.
2. Add quantity, review address, selected promotions, prices and delivery terms, then explicitly confirm the reviewed basket. The server freezes these terms. The success screen opens the order.
3. The same order appears in the wholesaler queue. Confirm it or propose a versioned product/quantity amendment. A pending amendment does not silently replace the contract; the merchant reviews before/after lines and total and explicitly accepts or refuses. Stale, expired or changed-stock terms cannot be accepted.
4. Warehouse preparation records actual quantities and lot scans. Dispatch assigns a named courier and manifest; custody transfer and departure remain separate from commercial confirmation.
5. The merchant sees confirmation, preparation, readiness, departure, receipt and payment independently. A courier location appears only after an actual authorized observation, with its timestamp and freshness. The 3D depot and replay are labelled illustrative.
6. Customer QR/quantity proof records actual accepted/refused units. Payment records method, amount and required reference. A click cannot fabricate cash, bank settlement or receipt. Driver cash and finance reconciliation remain independent.

## Campaigns, gifts and shops

The fictional preview provides one discount, one free-product campaign, one challenge and three illustrated rewards. Existing service rules still apply: approved terms, explicit selection, bounded stock, eligible delivered/paid progress and independent award review. Shop selection snapshots are filtered by region/name/phone and exclude other workspaces. Broadcasts remain consent-filtered in-app delivery. CSV export protects spreadsheet formula cells.

## Recall safety

A recall identifies the exact product, lot number and normalized expiry instant. The hold blocks catalogue availability, ordering, preparation, departure and positive delivery acceptance. Notices and acknowledgements do not represent physical recovery. Recovery either moves real unreserved depot units to quarantine or links an already physically received, identified return. Same-day expiry timestamps cannot change identity to evade containment. All-refused safe returns remain possible. Case closure retains the containment and makes residual/unknown scope explicit.
