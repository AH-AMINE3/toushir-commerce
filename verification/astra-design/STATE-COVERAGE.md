## Material findings and limits

- **Submission wording finding resolved in source and covered by the targeted checkout regression.** Independently re-read the root agent’s correction in `Merchant.tsx:1924`: SUBMITTED now says “Votre commande est reçue.” and explicitly awaits wholesaler confirmation before preparation. At `Merchant.tsx:2116`, REQUESTED/null totals remain “Total à confirmer”, SUBMITTED uses “Total transmis”, QUOTED/OFFER_DECLINED use “Total proposé”, and other priced states use neutral “Total de la commande”. No accidental discrepancy was found in these changed branches. [Targeted regression result](submission-final/expanded-browser.json), recorded 2026-10-17T16:11:40.812Z, lists four completed checks, zero failures and zero errors: seeded wholesale dashboard, merchant catalogue, direct checkout with merchant confirmation, and the same submitted order visible in wholesale operations. This verifies the regression’s scoped checkout path; it does not prove every status branch or full frame parity.
- **Distinct frame presentation is not complete by source inspection.** Checkout and submission recovery, amendment outcome pages, search, discrepancy reporting and the partial-reception summary are implemented as combined sections, notices or phases. Their business actions exist, but those standalone Figma state layouts cannot be called implemented one for one. M96 has quantity adjustment in the receipt check; this review did not establish a separate discrepancy-report form equivalent to the design frame.
- **No entire business workflow among these 41 states was found to lack a source path.** Browsing, cart submission/recovery, pending wholesaler confirmation, amendment decisions, delivery tracking, quantity approval, expiring receipt QR, payment display, gifts and overview chart selection have source paths. Conditional branches remain unverified at the individual-state level. This conclusion does not cover the design’s peripheral exits or the full application.
- **Reception and payment are correctly separated in the inspected source.** `MerchantDelivery.tsx:432` explicitly states QR approval records no payment, while `Merchant.tsx:2120` displays collected/remaining amounts and recorded payments. The actual driver handoff, cash collection, offline recovery and receipt-to-payment synchronization need their own runtime evidence.
- **Figma evidence is bounded too.** `final-audit.json.validationLimits` lists 11 visually inspected screen frames; it explicitly excludes exhaustive conditional playback, dark-screen visual validation and full application acceptance. Its zero broken internal destinations establish graph integrity, not source runtime fidelity. The ledger’s motion constants match the inspected cart animation source, but native animation playback has not been tested.
