## User tasks and first viewport

An administrator opens Portefeuilles & visites, selects a primary shop, assigns a named adviser with a reason, then plans contact/visit tasks. The first viewport keeps the commercial follow-up title, actual portfolio/shop selector, open tasks and qualification surface legible. Empty portfolios explain how to obtain an assignment. Each task retains assignee, due time, declared result and next action. Replanning, completion and cancellation preserve history. Contact and visit reports are explicitly self-reported; no call or physical attendance proof is fabricated.

A SALES adviser sees only currently assigned shops and the CRM navigation. Reassignment removes their former scope. An expired/revoked professional session returns to professional sign-in, without an automatic administrator demo. The actual person's identity is retained in records.

The merchant opens Mon Toushir → Demandes assistées. Owner/Manager reviews the named adviser, exact personal basket snapshot, address, note and bounded expiry, then explicitly checks consent. The authority covers one REQUEST_QUOTE only. Buyer/Viewer sees a clear read-only explanation. The adviser reviews the exact grant and submits one RFQ; they cannot edit its price/quantity/address/mode, accept the quote, receive products or pay for the merchant. The merchant basket remains intact. Unused grants can be revoked and cannot survive a changed address, issuer authority, assignment or primary-shop access.
